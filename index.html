8 Official Problem Statements

◆

Carefully curated by the AI&ML Department to reflect pressing technological, municipal, healthcare, and campus challenges. Choose your track and forge an impactful solution.

Theme 01 [S] Software
AI-Powered Urban Mobility

Intelligent Traffic Light Synchronization

Traffic congestion frequently occurs at busy intersections when traffic lights follow fixed timings without considering actual vehicle density. This leads to long queues, fuel wastage, and delays for emergency vehicles. Develop an intelligent system that analyses traffic density and coordinates nearby signals dynamically.

🏛️ Focus: Municipal Traffic & Emergency Corridors
Theme 02 [S] Software
Smart Education and Classroom Analytics

Academic Performance-Based Intelligent Seating Arrangement

Teachers find it difficult to arrange classroom seating that supports students with diverse learning curves. Develop an intelligent seating system that accepts student metrics, performance averages, and learning needs to generate an optimized, privacy-preserving seating plan encouraging peer learning.

🏫 Focus: Academic Institutions & Pedagogy
Theme 03 [S] Software
Smart Tourism and Public Safety

Crowd Management at Pilgrimage Sites

Large pilgrimages and religious gatherings attract thousands of visitors to locations with narrow entry routes. Authorities struggle to detect bottleneck zones early, risking stampedes. Develop a crowd management system estimating density, issuing timely alerts, and suggesting safe diversion routes.

🛡️ Focus: State Tourism & Pilgrimage Administration
Theme 04 [S] Software
Campus Grievance / Digital Trust

Grievance Cell Chatbot / Product Authentication

Students struggle to raise complaints across scattered, manual channels, while institutions also combat counterfeit credentials. Develop an automated LLM-powered grievance redressal chatbot system for college student welfare or a digital trust authentication platform verifying product & booklet authenticity.

🔐 Focus: Student Welfare / Institutional Verification
Theme 05 [S] Software
Preventive Healthcare and AI

Early Detection of Silent Diseases

Conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and anaemia often remain unnoticed until severe complications develop. Develop an AI-assisted screening assessment system that analyses preliminary health markers and patient records to recommend timely medical evaluations without claiming clinical replacement.

🩺 Focus: Primary Healthcare & Community Workers
Theme 06 [S] Software
Healthcare Cybersecurity

Ransomware Resilience for Hospitals

Hospitals depend on digital patient records and critical telemetry. A ransomware attack can paralyze life-saving operations. Develop a healthcare cybersecurity monitoring and incident preparedness system detecting suspicious file variations, verifying backup readiness, and orchestrating rapid recovery.

🏥 Focus: Hospital IT Administration / CERT-In
Theme 07 [S] Software
Smart Campus & Water Infrastructure

Smart Campus App / Water Infrastructure Leakage

Develop a comprehensive college management application that centralizes administrative operations (fees, performance checks, transportation), or an intelligent system predicting invisible campus water leakages through consumption patterns and pressure sensors.

💧 Focus: College Administration & Campus Maintenance
Theme 08 [S] Software
Smart Supply Chain and Food Safety

Food Cold-Chain Failure Prediction

Perishable food and biological supplies degrade rapidly when refrigeration thresholds are breached in transit. Develop a predictive monitoring engine analysing temperature logs, humidity sensors, and transit delays to flag high-risk shipments before irreversible spoilage occurs.

❄️ Focus: Food Safety Officers & Cold Logistics
